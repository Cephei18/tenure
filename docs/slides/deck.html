The problem

Curators are trusted with everything

Curators are fully trusted. Stream Finance, Nov 2025: curator vault deposits fell from ~$10B to ~$6.9B.
AI curators can be poisoned undetectably. Princeton, “AI Agents in Cryptoland”: memory injection works; detectors help little once stored context is corrupted.
A poisoned curator doesn't steal. It bleeds the vault with small, valid-looking decisions.

Today's tools

Static caps (Arrakis-style)Stop one big loss, miss many small ones.
Guardians & timelocks (Morpho, Yearn)Need a human awake.
Curator scorecardsRead after the damage.
Agent spending limits (Kusari)Limit what an agent does, not how well.
Tenure · UHI112
  • Depositors hand the curator everything. Stream showed what mandate drift costs: billions left within days.
  • AI curators add a new hole: poison the memory, and you can't tell by inspecting the agent.
  • The attack is a bleed: park liquidity at a bad price, trade against it. No theft transaction.
  • Static caps: every bleed step is under the cap. Guardians: nobody is awake for 25 small moves.
  • Scorecards: after the money is gone. Spending limits: they bound actions, not decision quality.
The insight
Don't detect the compromise.
Measure the damage.
Human, bot or poisoned AI: a harmful decision is harmful whoever made it.
HumanBotPoisoned AI →same decision, same grade
Tenure · UHI113
  • Everyone else asks: is this agent compromised? That question has no reliable answer.
  • Tenure asks a question we can answer on-chain: did this decision lose depositors money?
  • When the damage adds up, Tenure takes freedom away automatically. Good decisions earn it back.
How Tenure works

Four layers

Tenure · UHI114
  • Mandate: the bounds depositors agreed to. Changing them is timelocked, so they can leave first.
  • Scoring: the core. Every rebalance is graded at the moment it happens and again 50 blocks later.
  • Budget: a leaky bucket, 2% of NAV in the scenario. Below 50% RESTRICTED, below 20% FROZEN.
  • FROZEN is sticky. Only a timelocked guardian restore brings it back, and only to 35%.
  • Reputation is measured by the hook itself. And withdrawals never check the tier.
How it works

Tenure · UHI115
  • The vault opens a session: the hook snapshots holdings at the Chainlink fair price.
  • Liquidity moves happen inside unlock. Outside a session, the hook reverts any move on the vault's position.
  • endRebalance checks the mandate and charges immediate leakage to the budget.
  • 50 blocks later: compare the new position with doing nothing. Negative alpha is charged too.
  • Only a hook can do this: it gates modifyLiquidity, reads the PoolManager itself, and settles in afterSwap.

Tenure · UHI116
  • Markouts come due 50 blocks later. Settling one inside a swap costs that swapper about 146k gas.
  • So SettlerRC on Lasna watches the hook and calls the permissionless settleMany. Honest status: it reacts live; the callback itself is proven in tests.
  • Second: a poisoned curator often runs several vaults. FreezeRC relays FROZEN to its other vaults.
  • It can only freeze. It can never unfreeze or move funds, so a compromised relay can't hurt depositors.
  • Live: vault A froze at block 68,837,823, vault B two blocks later.

Tenure · UHI117
  • If grading used the pool's spot price, the attacker could push it for one block and fake a good grade.
  • So every score uses ChainlinkReferencePrice. Stale or bad answers revert. Live on pool B with ETH/USD and USDC/USD.
  • Grading costs 537,900 gas per rebalance. That's about $16 on mainnet at 10 gwei.
  • On Unichain it's about 1.6 cents, and 50 one-second blocks is under a minute. Swaps pay +17,205 gas, about 17%.
